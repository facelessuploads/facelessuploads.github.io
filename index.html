Faceless Uploads

Faceless Uploads is a private desktop tool used only by its owner to upload his own videos to his own YouTube channels, Maps Facts Daily and Strange Stories. It is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Uploads finished video files, with title, description and tags, to the owner's YouTube channel via the YouTube Data API.
  • Reads basic channel and video information to confirm uploads succeeded.

It requests only these Google scopes: youtube.upload and youtube.readonly. OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared or sold.
