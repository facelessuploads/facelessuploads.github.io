Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Faceless Uploads ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner to upload his own videos to his own YouTube channels (Maps Facts Daily, Strange Stories).

Google user data accessed

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload – to upload videos to the owner's channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly – to read channel and video details to confirm uploads.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to perform uploads the owner initiates. OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer. The app has no servers and keeps no copies of YouTube data beyond what is needed to complete an upload.

Sharing

No data is shared with, transferred to, or sold to any third party, and it is not used for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; locally stored tokens can be deleted from the owner's computer.
